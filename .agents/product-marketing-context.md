# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-03-13*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** The only app blocker that unlocks   with Scripture.
**What it does:** FaithLock is a faith-based screen time app blocker that uses Bible verses and scripture quizzes to create intentional friction before accessing distracting apps. Rather than simply blocking, it interrupts the dopamine loop by requiring users to read and engage with Scripture (15-second minimum) before gaining access — creating a spiritual discipline layer on top of technical blocking.
**Product category:** Digital Wellness / Screen Time Management (Faith-Based)
**Product type:** Mobile app (iOS-first, Flutter)
**Business model:** Freemium with tiered premium subscriptions (Weekly / Monthly / Yearly) via RevenueCat. Free trial available on most plans. Win-back promotions for lapsed users (48-hour eligibility window).

## Target Audience
**Target users:** Christian believers ages 16-45 — youth pastors, young professionals, students, church community members
**Primary use case:** Breaking phone/social media addiction through scripture-based friction instead of hard blocking
**Jobs to be done:**
- "I want to stop doomscrolling and replace that habit with something meaningful"
- "I need accountability for my screen time that aligns with my faith"
- "I want to build a daily scripture habit without it feeling forced"
**Use cases:**
- Morning routine transformation (replace doomscrolling with prayer/scripture)
- Social media detox with spiritual accountability
- Youth group / church community digital wellness
- Study/work focus sessions with faith-based blocking
- Bedtime phone discipline (stop late-night scrolling)
- Sabbath/prayer time complete device lock

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Christians struggle with phone addiction and feel guilty about excessive screen time, but generic app blockers feel punitive and don't address the spiritual dimension of the struggle.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic blockers (Opal, One Sec, Clearspace) just say "No" — they restrict but don't redirect
- Accountability apps (Covenant Eyes, Victory Shield) feel judgmental and surveillance-heavy
- Bible apps (Hallow, Glorify, Abide) don't address the screen time problem at all
- Willpower alone fails because the dopamine loop is too strong
**What it costs them:** Hours of daily screen time, guilt, weakened spiritual life, strained relationships, poor sleep, reduced productivity
**Emotional tension:** Shame cycle — scroll too much, feel guilty, promise to stop, fail again. Users feel disconnected from God and trapped by their devices.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Bible Mode (8.6K reviews), Pray Screen Time (8.9K reviews), Prayer Lock (12K reviews) — scripture-themed blockers but lack FaithLock's quiz-as-unlock mechanic, gamification depth, and mascot engagement
**Secondary:** Opal, Clearspace, One Sec, Forest — effective productivity blockers but completely secular, no spiritual framework
**Indirect:** Covenant Eyes, Victory Shield — accountability/monitoring approach that feels invasive and judgmental vs. FaithLock's grace-based friction

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Scripture-as-unlock mechanic (quiz engagement, not just reading)
- Spiritual battle framework (verses mapped to specific struggles: lust, fear, pride, anger, temptation)
- Gamification for faith (streaks, badges, rewards tied to spiritual growth)
- Judah mascot creating emotional connection and encouragement
- Grace-based approach — redirects rather than restricts
- "Liturgy" framing — positions as spiritual practice, not productivity hack
**How we do it differently:** Most blockers say "No." FaithLock says "Yes, but first..." — a 15-second scripture pause that disrupts the habit loop and replaces dopamine with Truth.
**Why that's better:** Users build real scripture knowledge while reducing screen time. The desire to scroll fades naturally instead of building resentment against a hard block.
**Why customers choose us:** "Finally an app that helps me use my phone less without making me feel guilty. It just redirects my attention to what matters."

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I'll just memorize the answers and skip through" | Dynamic quiz generation with multiple question types (fill-blank, multiple choice, reference) prevents gaming. The 15-second minimum ensures engagement regardless. |
| "Another subscription I don't need" | Free Forever plan available. Premium unlocks Sabbath Mode, Streak Freeze, and advanced analytics — but core blocking works free. |
| "I already have Screen Time built into iOS" | Apple Screen Time is easily bypassed and has no spiritual dimension. FaithLock creates intentional friction that transforms a restriction into a spiritual practice. |

**Anti-persona:** Users who want purely secular productivity tools. People seeking parental control/monitoring software. Users who want to completely eliminate phone usage (we redirect, not eliminate).

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Guilt from endless scrolling. Failed attempts with willpower alone. Generic blockers that feel punitive. Desire for spiritual growth that Bible apps alone don't fulfill.
**Pull:** Scripture-first approach feels meaningful, not restrictive. Gamification makes it engaging. Community of 14,000+ believers. Visible results (70% less screen time reported).
**Habit:** Familiarity with existing tools (iOS Screen Time, other blockers). Already paying for another service. "I'll just use willpower."
**Anxiety:** "Will it actually work or will I just bypass it?" "Is it worth paying for?" "Will it drain my battery?" "Will it be too preachy?"
