## Quick Verdict
- **Choose Holy Focus if**: You want the deepest prayer library available and a visually beautiful devotional experience
- **Choose Sanctum if**: You want emotional awareness, journaling, and Android support alongside your blocking

## Holy Focus in 30 Seconds

Holy Focus replaces distracting app screens with handpicked prayers, Bible verses, and custom faith-based icons. With over 300 prayer-and-verse combinations, each blocked-app intervention feels like opening a devotional card. The visual design is polished — one of the most aesthetically pleasing faith-based apps available. It's iOS-only, freemium at roughly $6.49/month, and holds a 4.8-star rating. The experience is passive: you see the prayer, you're inspired, and you choose to move on.

## Sanctum in 30 Seconds

Sanctum blocks distracting apps with its "Sacred Pause" — a Bible verse and prayer prompt — but goes deeper with emotional check-ins. "The Altar" asks how you're feeling and delivers a daily verse matched to your mood. Built-in journaling, mood logging, and an AI Bible chat feature make it more of a spiritual wellness app with blocking built in. It's one of the rare faith-based blockers on both iOS and Android, uses a freemium model, and holds 4.5+ stars.

## Key Differences

### Prayer Depth vs Emotional Breadth
Holy Focus goes deep on one thing: prayer. 300+ handpicked prayers means months of unique content every time you try to open a distraction. Sanctum goes wide — prayer, journaling, mood tracking, AI chat, verse matching. If you want the richest prayer experience available, Holy Focus wins. If you want a broader spiritual toolkit, Sanctum covers more ground.

### Visual Experience
Holy Focus treats every blocked-app intervention as a design opportunity. Custom icons, carefully paired verses and prayers, devotional-card layouts. Sanctum is visually clean but doesn't prioritize aesthetics the same way. For users who respond to beauty and visual calm, Holy Focus creates a more meditative interruption.

### Android Availability
Sanctum works on Android. Holy Focus doesn't. For the roughly 45% of US smartphone users on Android (and the vast majority worldwide), this alone makes the decision.

### Self-Knowledge Features
Sanctum's mood logging creates a pattern over time. After a few weeks, you can see when you reach for your phone most (mornings? after work?), what emotional state triggers it (boredom? stress?), and whether your habits are changing. Holy Focus doesn't track any of this — it's purely a moment-by-moment intervention with no longitudinal data.

## Which Should You Choose?

**You want the best prayer-focused experience**: Holy Focus. Nothing else in this space matches its curated prayer library and visual design.

**You use your phone to escape emotions you'd rather not feel**: Sanctum. The mood check-in catches the emotional trigger behind the habit, not just the behavior.

**You're on Android**: Sanctum. Full stop.

**You want to track your patterns over time**: Sanctum. Its journaling and mood logging build a picture of your phone habits that Holy Focus can't replicate.

**You prefer simplicity and beauty over features**: Holy Focus. It does one thing — prayer-based app interruption — and does it with more care and polish than any competitor.

**You're interested in AI-assisted Bible study**: Sanctum. Its AI Bible chat can help explore passages and find cross-references, though opinions vary on how useful AI is for Bible study.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do Holy Focus prayers repeat often?**
With 300+ unique prayer-and-verse combinations, most users report weeks or months before seeing repeats, assuming they're using the app regularly. The library continues to grow with updates.

**Can I turn off Sanctum's AI features?**
Yes. The AI Bible chat is entirely optional. You can use Sanctum for Sacred Pause blocking, mood check-ins, and journaling without ever opening the AI feature.

**Which app blocks more effectively?**
Holy Focus has slightly stronger blocking mechanics. Sanctum's Sacred Pause is more of an intentional interruption. If bypassing the block is a concern, Holy Focus edges ahead.

**Is there a faith-based blocker that combines prayer depth with active engagement?**
FaithLock pairs Bible verse quizzes (active engagement) with prayer reminders and scheduled blocking, offering a middle ground between Holy Focus's prayer depth and more interactive approaches.

**Do either of these apps support family sharing?**
Neither currently offers dedicated family plans. Both are designed as individual self-accountability tools. For family screen time management, Apple's Family Sharing or dedicated parental control apps are better options.
