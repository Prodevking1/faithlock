## Quick Verdict
- **Choose FaithLock if**: You want app blocking tied to Scripture engagement and spiritual practice
- **Choose Forest if**: You want gamified focus sessions with a visual reward system and real tree planting

## FaithLock in 30 Seconds

FaithLock blocks distracting apps and requires Bible verse engagement to unlock them. The quiz-based system draws from 31,000+ verses in the Berean Standard Bible. A 30-day spiritual covenant adds accountability, while scheduled lock times and prayer reminders structure your day. It's designed for Christians who want their phone habits to become spiritual practice. iOS-only, free tier available, premium at $24.99/year.

## Forest in 30 Seconds

Forest gamifies focus time. Start a session, and a virtual tree begins growing. If you leave the app or open a distraction, the tree dies. Complete the session, and you earn coins — which can be spent to plant real trees through the Trees for the Future partnership. The concept is simple, visual, and oddly compelling. Over 50 million users have made Forest one of the most popular focus apps globally. Available on iOS, Android, and as a Chrome extension. One-time purchase on iOS (~$3.99), free on Android with ads.

## Key Differences

### Philosophy: Spiritual Growth vs Environmental Gamification
FaithLock's reward is spiritual — you engage with God's Word as part of managing your phone. Forest's reward is visual and environmental — you grow trees on screen and fund real tree planting in the world. Both give you something positive during the restriction, but they tap into completely different motivations.

### Blocking Model
FaithLock blocks specific apps and requires a quiz to unlock them. Forest blocks everything during a timed session — you start a 25-minute focus session, and any phone use kills your tree. These are different use cases. FaithLock is always-on protection against specific distracting apps. Forest is session-based focus for work or study periods.

### Cost Structure
Forest's iOS app is a one-time ~$3.99 purchase. FaithLock is subscription-based at $24.99/year. For sheer cost-effectiveness, Forest wins. But they serve different purposes, so direct price comparison is slightly misleading.

### Cross-Platform Availability
Forest works everywhere — iOS, Android, Chrome. FaithLock is iOS-only. If you switch between devices or are on Android, Forest covers more ground.

## Which Should You Choose?

**You want spiritual discipline tied to screen time**: FaithLock. Forest can't replicate Bible engagement.

**You want to stay focused during work or study sessions**: Forest. Its timer-based sessions are built for productivity blocks.

**You need always-on app blocking, not just focus sessions**: FaithLock. It blocks apps all day, not just during timed sessions.

**You're motivated by visible progress and gamification**: Forest. The growing tree, coin system, and real tree planting are deeply satisfying for gamification-responsive users.

**You're on a tight budget**: Forest's one-time ~$3.99 is hard to beat.

**You want cross-platform support**: Forest. iOS, Android, Chrome. FaithLock is iOS-only.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use Forest for all-day app blocking like FaithLock?**
Not really. Forest is designed for timed focus sessions (typically 25-120 minutes). You can run back-to-back sessions, but it's not built as an always-on blocker. FaithLock blocks specific apps continuously until you engage with Scripture.

**Does Forest have any faith-based features?**
No. Forest is entirely secular. Its motivational framework is environmental (plant real trees) and gamification (grow virtual forest). There's no Scripture, prayer, or spiritual content.

**Which app reduces screen time more effectively?**
They reduce different types of screen time. FaithLock reduces mindless app-opening throughout the day. Forest reduces phone use during designated focus periods. The right choice depends on whether your problem is constant app-checking or inability to focus during specific times.

**Can I use both apps for different purposes?**
Yes, actually. Since Forest is session-based and FaithLock is app-based, they can coexist — use Forest for work focus sessions and FaithLock for ongoing app management. This combination works better than running two always-on blockers.

**Has Forest actually planted real trees?**
Yes. Forest partners with Trees for the Future and reports planting millions of real trees funded by user coin purchases. The real-world impact is verified and ongoing.
