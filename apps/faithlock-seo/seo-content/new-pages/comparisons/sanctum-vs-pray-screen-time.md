## Quick Verdict
- **Choose Sanctum if**: You want a full spiritual wellness experience with journaling, mood tracking, and AI features
- **Choose Pray Screen Time if**: You want a free, no-frills prayer-based blocker — especially on Android

## Sanctum in 30 Seconds

Sanctum intercepts distracting apps with a "Sacred Pause" — a Bible verse, prayer prompt, and emotional check-in. Its "Altar" feature matches daily verses to your mood. Built-in journaling, mood logging over time, and an AI Bible chat feature make it the most feature-rich faith-based blocker available. It works on both iOS and Android, uses a freemium model, and holds 4.5+ stars.

## Pray Screen Time in 30 Seconds

Pray Screen Time has a straightforward pitch: block apps, pray before unlocking. Every morning, a reminder nudges you to start your day with prayer, plus you get a daily Bible verse. There's no gamification, no streaks, no elaborate devotional system. It's one of the few faith-based blockers on Android with a genuinely usable free tier — not a crippled demo. Rated 4.9 stars, available on iOS and Android.

## Key Differences

### Feature Richness vs Simplicity
This is the fundamental split. Sanctum is a spiritual wellness platform that happens to block apps. Pray Screen Time is an app blocker that happens to include prayer. If you want mood tracking, journaling, AI Bible conversations, and personalized verse delivery, Sanctum is the only option. If you just want your apps blocked with a prayer before unlocking, Pray Screen Time does that without the overhead.

### Free Tier Quality
Pray Screen Time's free tier is genuinely functional. You can block apps and pray before unlocking without paying anything. Sanctum's free tier is more limited — the best features (mood matching, full journaling, AI chat) sit behind the paywall. For users who can't or won't pay for a subscription, Pray Screen Time is the clear winner.

### Stability and Polish
Sanctum is the more polished app with a more modern interface. However, some Pray Screen Time users have reported technical issues — getting stuck in demo loops, needing to restart in safe mode. The 4.9-star rating suggests most users have a smooth experience, but the technical hiccups are worth knowing about. Sanctum tends to run more reliably across devices.

### Approach to Scripture
Sanctum personalizes Scripture delivery based on your emotional state. Feeling anxious? You get a verse about peace. Feeling grateful? You get a verse about praise. Pray Screen Time delivers a daily verse without emotional context. The personalization is meaningful if you believe Scripture can speak differently depending on where you are emotionally.

## Which Should You Choose?

**You want a free, working app blocker with prayer**: Pray Screen Time. Its free tier is the most usable in the entire faith-based blocker market.

**You want to understand why you reach for your phone**: Sanctum. Mood logging over weeks reveals patterns — when you're most vulnerable, what emotions drive the habit.

**You want the simplest possible setup**: Pray Screen Time. Download, select apps to block, pray when prompted. Done.

**You're interested in AI-assisted Bible study**: Sanctum. No other faith-based blocker offers this feature.

**You've had technical issues with other apps**: Sanctum tends to be more stable. Pray Screen Time works well for most users but has known edge cases with demo mode loops.

**You're on a tight budget on Android**: Pray Screen Time. Free, functional, and available on your platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Pray Screen Time really completely free?**
The core blocking and prayer features are free and functional. There are premium features available, but unlike many "freemium" apps, the free tier isn't a stripped-down demo.

**Does Sanctum's AI Bible chat give accurate theological answers?**
The AI chat is a study tool, not a theologian. It can help explore context, find cross-references, and explain passages. But like any AI, it can occasionally produce inaccurate or superficial interpretations. Use it as a starting point for study, not a substitute for pastoral guidance.

**Which app is better for Android specifically?**
Both work on Android, but the experience can differ by device. Pray Screen Time's simpler architecture means fewer compatibility issues across Android manufacturers. Sanctum's more complex features occasionally behave differently on older Android versions.

**Can I export my Sanctum journal entries?**
Check the current version for export options. Journaling apps that lock your data without export should be approached cautiously — your spiritual reflections belong to you.

**Is there a faith-based blocker with stronger blocking on Android?**
Both Sanctum and Pray Screen Time offer moderate blocking strength on Android. Android's more open architecture makes hard blocking more difficult than on iOS. For the strongest faith-based blocking, iOS-only apps like FaithLock or Bible Mode use Apple's Family Controls framework, which is harder to bypass.
