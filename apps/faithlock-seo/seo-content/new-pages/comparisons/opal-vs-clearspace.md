## Quick Verdict
- **Choose Opal if**: You want the best analytics in screen time management with extremely hard blocking
- **Choose Clearspace if**: You want customizable delay timers and cognitive challenges (math problems, typing tasks) as friction

## Opal in 30 Seconds

Opal combines AI-powered screen time analytics with strong blocking. It tracks your usage in granular detail, identifies patterns with AI insights, and enforces limits through "Deep Focus" sessions that are notoriously difficult to bypass. Social features include Gem sessions and team challenges. Over 1 million users. iOS-only, freemium at ~$59.99/year.

## Clearspace in 30 Seconds

Clearspace puts deliberate obstacles between you and distracting apps. When you try to open a blocked app, you face a delay timer — sometimes paired with a cognitive challenge like solving a math problem or completing a typing exercise. The friction is designed to be just annoying enough that you reconsider. Usage insights and group challenges round out the feature set. iOS-only, freemium at ~$69.99/year.

## Key Differences

### Total Lockout vs Deliberate Friction
Opal's Deep Focus completely blocks access. Your apps are gone until the session ends. Clearspace puts a barrier in front of the app — a timer, a math problem — then lets you through. Opal is a wall. Clearspace is a speed bump. If you need total restriction, Opal. If you want to slow yourself down and make opening the app a conscious decision, Clearspace.

### Analytics Quality
Opal's AI-driven analytics are the gold standard. App-by-app breakdowns, daily/weekly trends, usage pattern identification, goal tracking. Clearspace offers usage insights but doesn't approach Opal's analytical depth. If data motivates your behavior change, Opal gives you significantly more to work with.

### The Challenge System
Clearspace's cognitive challenges are unique. Having to solve 12 x 7 before you can open TikTok is genuinely annoying — and that's the point. No other major blocker uses this approach. The challenges are customizable: choose math, typing, or just a timer delay. Opal doesn't offer anything comparable because its philosophy is total blocking, not friction.

### Cost
Both are premium-priced. Clearspace at ~$69.99/year is slightly more expensive than Opal at ~$59.99/year. For the price difference, Opal arguably delivers more value through its superior analytics.

## Which Should You Choose?

**You want the best analytics available**: Opal. Its AI insights are unmatched.

**You want to slow yourself down, not lock yourself out**: Clearspace. The challenge system creates friction without total restriction.

**You need the hardest blocking possible**: Opal's Deep Focus.

**You find the idea of math problems before Instagram satisfying**: Clearspace. The cognitive challenges are uniquely effective at making you question whether you really need to check that app.

**Budget is tight between these two**: Opal is $10/year cheaper and offers deeper analytics.

**You want social accountability**: Both offer group features, but Opal's Gem sessions are more developed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do Clearspace's math problems actually deter people?**
Yes. The key insight is that solving a cognitive task activates your prefrontal cortex — the same brain region responsible for impulse control. By the time you've solved 15 x 8, you're no longer in autopilot mode and can make a conscious decision about whether you really need to open the app.

**Can you make Opal's Deep Focus even harder to bypass?**
Deep Focus is already extremely hard to bypass. Some users report it's virtually impossible without deleting the app entirely — which requires your Apple ID password and a waiting period.

**Which app is better for someone who works from home?**
Opal's scheduled sessions are great for creating work/non-work boundaries. Clearspace's challenge system works well for quick checks — you can still access work tools but face friction on distractions. Choose based on whether you need total restriction during work hours (Opal) or just extra friction (Clearspace).

**Are there cheaper alternatives that work nearly as well?**
ScreenZen offers waiting periods and analytics at a lower price point. One Sec provides breathing pauses for less than either Opal or Clearspace. For users wanting a faith-based approach, FaithLock uses Bible verse quizzes at $24.99/year.

**Can I use Opal and Clearspace together?**
Not reliably on iOS. Both use the Screen Time API, and running two blockers creates conflicts.
