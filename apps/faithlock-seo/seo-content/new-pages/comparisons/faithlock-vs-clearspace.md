## Quick Verdict
- **Choose FaithLock if**: You want your screen time battles to become moments of Scripture engagement
- **Choose Clearspace if**: You want science-backed friction through delays and cognitive challenges without a faith component

## FaithLock in 30 Seconds

FaithLock blocks distracting apps and unlocks them through Bible verse engagement. You read a verse and complete a quiz — no skimming and tapping through. The complete BSB library (31,000+ verses) powers the content. A 30-day covenant commitment feature adds accountability, while scheduled lock times and prayer reminders bring structure. iOS-only, free tier available, premium at $24.99/year.

## Clearspace in 30 Seconds

Clearspace places deliberate obstacles between you and your distracting apps. When you try to open a blocked app, you face a delay timer — sometimes with a cognitive challenge like a math problem or typing exercise. The idea is rooted in behavioral science: adding friction to automatic behaviors reduces their frequency. Clearspace also offers usage insights, customizable block schedules, and group challenges. iOS-only, freemium with premium around $69.99/year.

## Key Differences

### Spiritual Friction vs Cognitive Friction
Both apps create friction. FaithLock fills that friction with Scripture. Clearspace fills it with cognitive tasks. The question is what kind of speed bump you want between you and Instagram. Bible engagement or math problems. For Christians, FaithLock turns an annoying interruption into a spiritual practice. Clearspace treats the interruption as a deterrent — something annoying enough to make you reconsider.

### Cost
The price gap is significant. FaithLock runs $24.99/year. Clearspace runs roughly $69.99/year — nearly three times more. Clearspace's higher price point reflects a larger development team and broader feature set, but for users who would be satisfied with either, the savings are real.

### Content Value
FaithLock's intervention has value on its own — you're reading Scripture. Even if you proceed to the blocked app after, you've spent 20-30 seconds with God's Word. Clearspace's intervention is purely a deterrent — a math problem has no inherent value beyond making you reconsider. If you want the friction itself to be meaningful, FaithLock wins clearly.

### Group Accountability
Clearspace's group challenges let you compete or collaborate with friends on screen time goals. FaithLock's community features are still developing. If social motivation drives you more than solo discipline, Clearspace currently offers more here.

## Which Should You Choose?

**You want the interruption itself to have meaning**: FaithLock. Bible verses create spiritual value. Math problems just create annoyance.

**You respond well to peer competition and group challenges**: Clearspace. Its social features are more developed.

**You want the strongest possible deterrent**: Clearspace. Having to solve a math problem is more annoying than reading a verse, which may deter opening the app in the first place.

**Budget matters**: FaithLock at $24.99/year is a fraction of Clearspace's ~$69.99/year.

**You're a Christian wanting to integrate faith into daily habits**: FaithLock. No secular app replicates this.

**You want detailed usage analytics**: Clearspace offers more granular usage pattern data.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which app is harder to bypass?**
Both use Apple's Family Controls framework, so the underlying blocking mechanism is similar. Clearspace's longer delay timers can feel more discouraging. FaithLock's quiz requires genuine engagement. Neither is trivially bypassable.

**Does Clearspace's math problem approach actually reduce screen time?**
Yes — the principle of adding friction to automatic behaviors is well-established in behavioral science. Clearspace reports significant usage reduction, though exact figures vary by user.

**Can I combine FaithLock with Clearspace?**
Not reliably. Both apps use the same iOS Screen Time API, and running two blockers creates conflicts.

**Is FaithLock's quiz easy to game?**
The quiz changes each time and draws from 31,000+ verses, making it impractical to memorize answers. You need to actually read and engage with the verse to proceed.

**Are there other faith-based alternatives with group features?**
Sanctum offers some community elements on both iOS and Android, though its blocking is less aggressive than either FaithLock or Clearspace.
