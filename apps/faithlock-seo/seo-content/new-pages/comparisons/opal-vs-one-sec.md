## Quick Verdict
- **Choose Opal if**: You want powerful AI-driven analytics and the hardest-to-bypass blocking on the market
- **Choose One Sec if**: You want a simple, psychology-backed breathing pause that reduces mindless app-opens without hard blocking

## Opal in 30 Seconds

Opal combines deep screen time analytics with strong blocking. AI-powered insights break down your phone usage — which apps, how often, at what times, and how your patterns shift. "Deep Focus" sessions create blocking that's extremely hard to bypass. Social features like Gem sessions let you challenge friends. Over 1 million users. iOS-only, freemium with premium at ~$9.99/month or $59.99/year.

## One Sec in 30 Seconds

One Sec takes one simple idea and executes it well: before you open a distracting app, you take a conscious breath. A breathing animation plays, then the app asks "Do you still want to open this?" One Sec reports that this pause alone reduces app-opens by 57%. Custom interventions, journaling options, and saved-time tracking add depth. iOS and Android, freemium with premium at ~$39.99/year.

## Key Differences

### Hard Block vs Mindful Nudge
Opal locks you out. Deep Focus sessions make your blocked apps inaccessible, period. One Sec pauses you, asks if you really want to continue, and lets you decide. These philosophies serve different problems. If you can't trust yourself to make the right choice in the moment, Opal removes the choice. If your problem is autopilot behavior rather than lack of willpower, One Sec's pause breaks the cycle while respecting your autonomy.

### Analytics Depth
Opal's analytics are in a different league. AI-driven insights about your usage patterns, app-by-app breakdowns, daily/weekly trends, comparison against your own goals. One Sec tracks time saved and reduction percentages but doesn't offer the same granular data. If understanding your phone habits in detail is a priority, Opal provides far more.

### Platform Coverage
One Sec works on Android. Opal doesn't. For Android users, One Sec is the option.

### Effectiveness Approach
Opal measures effectiveness by blocking: how many hours you stayed off blocked apps. One Sec measures by behavior change: how often you chose not to open an app after the pause. The 57% reduction metric is compelling because it reflects genuine decision-making, not forced restriction.

## Which Should You Choose?

**You want the strongest blocking available**: Opal. Deep Focus is one of the hardest blocks to bypass.

**You want a lighter touch that respects your autonomy**: One Sec. The breathing pause is enough for most people to reconsider.

**You want detailed analytics about your phone use**: Opal. Its AI insights are the best in the consumer market.

**You're on Android**: One Sec.

**You want the cheaper option**: One Sec at ~$39.99/year vs Opal's ~$59.99/year.

**You respond better to data than restrictions**: Opal's analytics can motivate change through awareness. One Sec's saved-time metrics are simpler but effective.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is One Sec's 57% reduction claim real?**
One Sec publishes this figure based on aggregated user data. Independent reports from users generally support significant reductions, though individual results vary. The underlying mechanism — a conscious pause before an automatic action — is supported by behavioral psychology research.

**Can Opal's Deep Focus be bypassed?**
Opal specifically designed Deep Focus to resist bypass attempts. It's one of the most resilient blocking systems available. You can still access essential apps (phone, messages) but your blocked apps stay blocked until the session ends.

**Which app works better for social media addiction specifically?**
Both work well. Opal is better if you need social media completely blocked during certain periods. One Sec is better if your issue is reflexive, habitual checking — the pause breaks the autopilot loop without requiring total lockout.

**Can I use both Opal and One Sec together?**
Running two blocking apps on iOS creates API conflicts. On Android (where only One Sec is available), the question doesn't arise.

**Is there an app that combines Opal's analytics with One Sec's mindful approach?**
Not exactly. ScreenZen comes closest by combining waiting periods with usage analytics. For users wanting a spiritual dimension, FaithLock adds Bible verse engagement to the blocking experience.
