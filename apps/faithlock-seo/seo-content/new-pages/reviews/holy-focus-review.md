## What Is Holy Focus?

Holy Focus calls itself "the first Christian screen time app that replaces distraction with daily prayer." When you try to open a blocked app, you don't see a countdown timer or a restriction notice. You see a handpicked prayer, a Bible verse, and a beautifully designed faith-based icon.

The difference between Holy Focus and most blockers is emphasis. Other apps use prayer or Scripture as the mechanism to unlock your phone. Holy Focus treats the prayer itself as the point. The blocking is almost secondary — it's the delivery system for a prayer habit.

If you've ever tried those "prayer of the day" apps that you forget to open by 9am, Holy Focus solves that problem by tying your prayers to the moments you're most likely to be on your phone. You'll never miss a prayer prompt because it shows up exactly when you reach for the thing you're trying to avoid.

## How It Works

1. **Download** Holy Focus from the App Store
2. **Select apps to block** — the usual suspects (social media, games, news)
3. **Grant Screen Time permissions** through Apple's Family Controls
4. When you open a blocked app, Holy Focus presents a **prayer screen**
5. The screen includes a **curated prayer**, a **Bible verse**, and a **custom icon**
6. **Read and reflect** on the prayer, then your apps unlock
7. Content **rotates daily**, so you see fresh prayers and verses each day

The unlock isn't quiz-based or scan-based — you read the prayer and continue. This is a deliberate design choice. Holy Focus trusts you to actually engage with the content rather than forcing verification. Whether that's a strength or weakness depends on your self-discipline (more on that below).

## Features Breakdown

**300+ Handpicked Prayers**
This is the crown jewel. The prayer library is the deepest of any faith-based blocker available. These aren't AI-generated or randomly pulled from a database. The team has curated prayers spanning topics like anxiety, gratitude, patience, temptation, family, work, and rest. Each one is paired with a relevant Scripture passage.

**Custom Faith Icons**
Every prayer screen comes with a unique hand-designed icon — crosses, doves, flames, open Bibles, praying hands. It's a small touch that makes a big difference. The screens feel like art, not software.

**Daily Rotation**
Content changes daily, so you're not seeing the same prayer every time you try to open TikTok. Over a year of use, you'll cycle through the entire library multiple times, but the rotation keeps things fresh enough that it doesn't feel repetitive within a given week.

**Visual Design**
This is where Holy Focus genuinely stands apart from every competitor. The UI is gorgeous. Soft colors, elegant typography, thoughtful spacing. Each blocked-app screen looks like something you'd frame, not something you'd rush past. If aesthetics matter to you — and for many people, they directly impact whether they stick with an app — Holy Focus wins this category hands down.

**App Blocking**
System-level blocking through Apple's Screen Time API. Solid, hard to bypass, same infrastructure that Bible Mode and FaithLock use. You select which apps to block and the blocking is consistent and reliable.

**Category-Based Prayers**
Some prayers are tagged by topic, so you can occasionally encounter prayers relevant to why you're reaching for your phone. Feeling anxious? The prayer addresses anxiety. Bored? The prayer speaks to contentment. This isn't as personalized as mood-matching apps like Prayer Lock, but it adds relevance.

## Who Is Holy Focus Best For?

**Christians who want to build a prayer habit.** If your spiritual life is heavy on Bible reading but light on prayer, Holy Focus addresses that specific gap. Every app interruption becomes a prayer moment.

**Aesthetics-driven users.** If ugly apps make you uninstall them within a day (you know who you are), Holy Focus is the only faith-based blocker that genuinely looks premium. The design quality matters for long-term retention.

**People who respond to beauty over rules.** Some people need hard accountability — quizzes, streaks, consequences. Others respond better to gentle, beautiful invitations. If you're in the second camp, Holy Focus speaks your language.

**Those who appreciate curated content.** If you're the type who prefers a curated Spotify playlist over shuffle-everything, the handpicked prayer library will feel right. Each prayer is there for a reason.

## Who Should Look Elsewhere?

**People who skim and skip.** Be honest with yourself. If you'll glance at the prayer for half a second and tap through to Instagram, Holy Focus won't help you. You need something with forced engagement — a quiz, a scanning requirement, something that slows you down.

**Budget-conscious users.** At $6.49/month, Holy Focus is one of the pricier options. If money is tight, Pray Screen Time is free and functional, and FaithLock's annual plan is half the cost.

**Android users.** iOS only. No workarounds.

**Anyone who wants data and tracking.** Holy Focus doesn't tell you how many times you were blocked, how much screen time you saved, or how long your prayer streak is. If numbers motivate you, look elsewhere.

**People who need scheduling.** You can't block apps only during certain hours. It's always-on.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Holy Focus worth $6.49/month?**
That depends on how you value prayer prompts. If Holy Focus becomes your primary way of maintaining a daily prayer habit — replacing a standalone prayer app you'd pay for anyway — then yes. If you just want basic app blocking and the prayers are secondary, cheaper options exist. Try the free tier first and see if the prayers meaningfully change your day before committing.

**Can I just read the prayer quickly and skip through?**
Technically, yes. Holy Focus doesn't force active engagement — no quizzes, no timers, no scanning. It trusts you to engage honestly. For disciplined users, this is respectful. For those who struggle with self-control (which is... presumably why you're downloading an app blocker), this is a real weakness.

**How often do prayers repeat?**
With 300+ prayers in the library, you'll see new content for months before things start cycling. Daily rotation means you get a fresh prayer each day. If you're only blocking 2-3 apps, you'll see the same prayer across all of them on a given day, but it changes the next day.

**Does Holy Focus work with Apple Screen Time?**
Yes. It uses Apple's Screen Time API (Family Controls framework) under the hood, so it integrates with the system-level blocking. Your existing Apple Screen Time settings remain active alongside Holy Focus.

**Can I request specific prayer topics?**
Not currently. The prayer library is curated by the team, and you can't filter or select specific topics. Some category-based matching exists, but you can't say "only show me prayers about anxiety." This is a feature several users have requested.

**Will it drain my battery?**
No. Like all apps using Apple's Screen Time API, Holy Focus has minimal battery impact — well under 1% per day.

**Is there a family plan or shared subscription?**
Not at this time. Each user needs their own subscription. For families with multiple iPhones, this cost adds up quickly — $6.49 per person per month.

**How does it compare to free prayer apps?**
Prayer apps like Echo or PrayerMate deliver daily prayers, but they rely on you remembering to open them. Holy Focus delivers prayers at the exact moment you're reaching for a distraction — that timing is what makes the habit stick. You're paying for the delivery mechanism as much as the content.
