## What Is Truple?

Truple, from Truple LLC, is best understood as two distinct tools sold under one brand. The first is a DNS filter: it blocks access to more than two million adult sites by routing your device's traffic through a dedicated DNS server, installed via a configuration profile on iOS. The second is a screenshot-based accountability app: it captures random screenshots of your entire device, runs them through AI to flag sexual content, and shares the flagged images with a trusted partner you've chosen.

The DNS filter genuinely blocks something. The accountability app does not; its entire strength depends on a human being reviewing the screenshots it shares. On iOS specifically, there's no single native Truple app; you use separate companion apps (Best Accountability for individual use, ScreenKin for families) that the company describes as powered by Truple.

This Truple review keeps those two products separate throughout, since conflating them is the easiest way to misunderstand what you're actually buying.

## How It Works

**DNS Filter**
1. **Sign up** and configure the DNS filter, using a configuration profile on iOS or DNS settings on other platforms
2. Your device's **traffic routes through Truple's dedicated DNS server**
3. Attempts to reach any of the **over two million flagged adult sites are blocked** at the network level
4. Changing your device's DNS settings can bypass the filter, unless the device is supervised or locked

**Accountability App**
1. **Install** the appropriate companion app: Best Accountability (individual) or ScreenKin (family) on iOS, or the native app on Android, Windows, macOS, ChromeOS, or Linux
2. **Invite a trusted partner** to receive your reports
3. The app **captures random screenshots** of your device in the background (via the screen-broadcast permission on iOS)
4. **AI analyzes each screenshot** for sexual content
5. **Flagged screenshots are shared** with your partner, along with email alerts when the filter itself is turned on or off

## Features Breakdown

**Two Million Plus Site DNS Filter**
Blocks access to a large, maintained list of adult sites at the network level, working even in private or incognito browsing modes since it operates below the browser.

**Random Screenshot Capture**
The accountability app periodically screenshots your entire device, not just a single app, giving broader coverage than tools that only monitor one browser.

**AI Content Flagging**
Screenshots are analyzed by AI to detect sexual content before being shared, rather than sending every single screenshot to your partner.

Truple claims this as a differentiator in this market, protecting the screenshots in transit.

**Device-Admin Locking (Android)**
Makes the app harder to uninstall on Android by requiring device-admin permissions to be revoked first.

**Email Alerts on Filter Toggle**
Your partner is notified by email whenever the DNS filter is turned on or off, adding visibility beyond the screenshot reports.

## Truple Review: DNS Filter or Accountability App, Which One Do You Need?

This is the single most useful thing any Truple review can clarify. If what you actually want is content made inaccessible, that's the DNS filter, and it's a real technical block, provided the device is locked or supervised so the DNS settings themselves can't be casually changed. If what you want is a trusted partner seeing your device activity, that's the accountability app, and it's a reporting tool, not a restriction. Buying one expecting the other's function is the most common way to end up disappointed with Truple.
