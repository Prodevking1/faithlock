## The Best Christian Devotional App in 2026: 6 Top Picks

### 1. YouVersion

**Best for**: Most people who want a free devotional app with an enormous library of reading plans

YouVersion, from Life.Church, offers reading plans and devotionals alongside its full Bible library, all free with no ads and no in-app purchases. Its social features let you read a plan alongside friends and compare progress.

**What stands out**: The devotional plan library is enormous and constantly updated, and it costs nothing at any tier.

**Where it falls short**: Because YouVersion covers so much ground (translations, plans, social features, video), the devotional experience is broader but less specialized than a purpose-built devotional app like First 5 or She Reads Truth.

| Price | Free, no ads, no in-app purchases |
| Platforms | iOS, Android, Web |
| Standout feature | Huge, constantly updated devotional plan library |

### 2. Our Daily Bread

**Best for**: Readers who want a completely free, no-paywall devotional with decades of institutional history

Our Daily Bread Ministries has published devotional content for 85+ years, and the app reflects that: a daily devotional in text and audio, an in-app Bible with 20+ languages, a Bible in a Year plan with full archive access, and an Evening Meditations podcast. There are no in-app purchases listed on its App Store page.

**What stands out**: It's entirely free with no paywall anywhere, and the multilingual support goes further than most devotional apps on this list.

**Where it falls short**: The App Store rating (around 3.9 stars) trails the newer, more design-forward apps on this list, and the interface is more utilitarian than something like She Reads Truth.

| Price | Free, no in-app purchases |

### 3. First 5

**Best for**: Women who want a short, structured daily habit rather than an open-ended reading session

First 5, from Proverbs 31 Ministries, delivers a roughly 5-minute daily Scripture teaching, studied book-by-book rather than a random verse of the day. It includes highlighting, note-taking, customizable reminders, and public or private community discussion groups.

**What stands out**: The "first five minutes of your day" framing and book-by-book structure give it more depth than a typical quick-devotional app, without requiring a long time commitment.

**Where it falls short**: The App Store rating sample size (883 ratings) is smaller than the larger apps on this list, and the content and community are built primarily around a women's audience.

### 4. She Reads Truth

**Best for**: Women who want daily reading plans with real-time community discussion

She Reads Truth offers the full Bible text in multiple translations plus daily devotional reading plans, with real-time community commenting synced between the app and website. It also supports highlighting, notes, and downloadable lock-screen Scripture images.

**What stands out**: The live community comments make it feel like a shared reading experience rather than a solitary app, which is uncommon in this category.

**Where it falls short**: The App Store rating (around 3.2 stars) is the lowest on this list. She Reads Truth also sells a separate print "Reading Guide" mail subscription at a different price than its $2.99/month in-app subscription; don't confuse the two when comparing costs.

### 6. Dwell

**Best for**: Anyone who wants their devotional read to them instead of reading it themselves

Dwell offers 75+ listening plans across 14 Bible translations, narrated by 20+ voices with text that follows along in sync, plus a sleep mode and scripture memorization tools.

**What stands out**: It's the only app on this list designed audio-first, which suits a devotional habit built around a commute or a bedtime routine rather than sitting down to read.

**Where it falls short**: Unlike every other app on this list, Dwell has no meaningful free tier past a trial period, starting at $9.99/month for a single user.

## Where FaithLock Fits

FaithLock isn't a devotional content library, and it doesn't compete with the reading plans above. What it does is turn the moments you'd normally spend on a distracting app into forced Scripture engagement instead.

FaithLock blocks distracting apps on iPhone using Apple's Family Controls (Screen Time) API and requires reading a Bible verse from the complete BSB library of 31,000+ verses and answering a quiz question about it before unlocking. You can set scheduled lock times around a morning devotion window, and the app tracks reading streaks with badges to reinforce the habit. A home screen widget also shows the verse of the day.

If your actual devotional habit keeps losing to your phone before you've opened your devotional app at all, FaithLock's Bible quiz interrupts that pattern directly. It's iOS only, and Premium runs $4.99/week or $24.99/year.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best Christian devotional app?**
YouVersion has the largest and most frequently updated library of devotional plans of any christian devotional app, which makes it the strongest all-purpose pick. Our Daily Bread is the best choice if a completely ad-free, paywall-free experience matters more than plan variety.

**Is there a free Christian devotional app with no subscription at all?**
Yes. YouVersion, Our Daily Bread, and First 5 are all free with no in-app purchases, per their App Store listings checked 2026-08-15.

**What's a good devotional app for a short daily habit?**
First 5 is built specifically around a roughly 5-minute daily teaching, studied book-by-book, which suits a habit you can complete before the rest of your day starts.

**What's the difference between She Reads Truth and He Reads Truth?**
They're companion apps from the same publisher, with the same structured reading-plan model but separate content built for a women's audience (She Reads Truth) and a men's audience (He Reads Truth).

**Is there a devotional app I can listen to instead of read?**
Dwell is built audio-first, with 20+ narrators reading across 14-15 translations and text that follows along in sync, plus a sleep mode.

**Does FaithLock replace a devotional app?**
No. FaithLock blocks distracting apps and requires answering a Bible verse quiz to unlock them; it doesn't offer devotional reading plans, community discussion, or commentary the way the apps above do. It works best alongside one of them, not instead of one.

**Which devotional app has the most community features?**
She Reads Truth stands out for real-time comments synced between its app and website, letting you read alongside a broader community rather than alone.

**Are any of these devotional apps completely free forever, not just a trial?**
Yes. YouVersion and Our Daily Bread have no in-app purchases at all. First 5 is also free. She Reads Truth, He Reads Truth, and Dwell offer free access to some content but reserve their full plan libraries for a paid subscription.

## Final Verdict

YouVersion is the right starting point for almost anyone: it's free, the plan library is enormous, and there's no paywall to run into later. If you specifically want a shorter, more focused daily habit, First 5's 5-minute format is a better fit than an open-ended plan library.

FaithLock doesn't change that recommendation. It solves the problem that happens before you ever open a devotional app: the phone reaching for your attention first. If that's the actual obstacle in your routine, it's worth pairing with whichever devotional app you pick from this list.
