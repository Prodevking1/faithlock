## What "Free" Means in This Category

Every app in this article shows a price of Free on its App Store listing. That figure describes the download and nothing else.

There are three real business models hiding behind that word, and knowing which one you are looking at saves an afternoon.

**Actually free.** No paid tier on the feature you want. The lock screen verse works forever without paying. In this category, that is essentially YouVersion and not much else.

**Free tier plus paywall.** The widget works, but the good designs, the translation choice, or the customization sit behind a subscription. Heavenly is the clearest example: the app functions on the free tier, and the design library that made you install it does not fully open until you pay.

**Free trial in disguise.** The app is usable for a short time, then the useful part closes. Devotional apps skew this way because their content is expensive to produce.

None of these is dishonest. But if your search was "free Bible verse app for lock screen," only the first category is answering your question.

## Where FaithLock Sits, Precisely

FaithLock comes up in lock screen searches, so it is worth being exact about what it does, because it is not a lock screen app.

**It has no lock screen widget.** Its widget is home screen only, in small and medium sizes, showing your prayer streak, your garden stage, and the verse of the day.

**Its verse moment is not a glance.** The verse appears when you try to open an app you have chosen to lock. You reach for Instagram, and Scripture is what loads instead, with a prompt to read and pray that the listing describes as taking about 30 seconds before the app opens.

That is a deliberately different bet. A lock screen verse asks nothing of you and is free to ignore. A verse standing between you and an app you were already opening is harder to skip, and more annoying, which is roughly the point.

On price: the app is free to download, with Premium Membership in-app purchases at $6.99 and $39.99. It is iOS 13.0 or later, and carries 4.2 out of 5 from 16 ratings, which is a small enough sample that the number should not carry much weight either way.

## The Free Option That Is Not an App

If the goal is simply Scripture on your lock screen at no cost, the most reliable route skips the App Store entirely: set a wallpaper that has the verse in the image.

**Why it often beats a widget:**

- **Nothing truncates.** Lock screen widgets get a narrow strip below the clock, which cuts most verses off mid-sentence. A wallpaper uses the whole screen.
- **Nothing to refresh.** No stale verse, no Background App Refresh setting, no widget showing yesterday.
- **It works on every phone.** iPhone, Android, any manufacturer, any version. Android in particular has no universal lock screen widget system, which makes this the only consistent answer there.
- **No subscription can ever appear.** It is an image file.

**The trade-off** is that it does not change on its own. You are choosing one verse until you swap it, which is a downside if you want daily variety and an upside if you are memorizing something.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best completely free Bible verse app for the lock screen?**
YouVersion. It is the only widely used option in this category with no premium tier at all, it works on iPhone and Android, and the Verse of the Day widget is included rather than upsold.

**Can I put a Bible verse on my lock screen without downloading an app?**
Yes, and it is often the better answer. Save a wallpaper image with the verse already in it and set it as your lock screen. Nothing truncates, nothing goes stale, and it works identically on every phone.

**Why do free Bible verse widgets cut off the verse?**
Because iOS gives lock screen widgets a small fixed area below the clock, and long verses do not fit. This is a space constraint, not a free-tier limitation, and paying does not fix it. A home screen widget in medium size, or a wallpaper, will show the full text.

**Is FaithLock a free Bible verse lock screen app?**
It is not a lock screen app. Its widget is home screen only, and its verse appears when you open an app you have locked rather than on the lock screen. The app is free to download with paid Premium Membership options at $6.99 and $39.99.

**Are free Bible verse apps full of ads?**
The major ones on this list are not ad-supported. They monetize through subscriptions instead, which is why the pattern is a free download with a paywall on the good features rather than banner ads on a free product.

**Does a free lock screen verse app work on Android?**
YouVersion does. Most of the design-led verse widget apps are iOS only, and Android has no universal lock screen widget system, so the wallpaper approach is more dependable there. Our [Android guide](/resources/how-to-add-bible-verse-widget-android) covers the details.
