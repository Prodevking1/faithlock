## Key Takeaways

- A lock screen Bible verse wallpaper is a static image you set once. A lock screen verse widget is a small block that auto-refreshes with a new verse, usually daily.
- You can run both at the same time: a verse wallpaper as the background image, plus a widget layered on top.
- iOS lets you create multiple Lock Screens, each with its own verse wallpaper and widget combination, and switch between them by Focus mode.
- Not every faith app puts a widget directly on your lock screen. Some, like FaithLock, show a verse at a different moment instead: when you try to open a blocked app.

---

## Quick Answer

To set a lock screen Bible verse wallpaper on iPhone, long-press your Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose Photos as your wallpaper source, and select a saved verse image. For an auto-refreshing verse instead of a static image, add a Bible app's widget to the space below the clock.

## Wallpaper vs Widget: What's the Difference

These get used interchangeably, but they're two different things on your lock screen.

A **lock screen wallpaper** is the background image itself. It's static; it doesn't change unless you manually swap it or set up an auto-rotation.

A **lock screen widget** is a small interactive block, usually sitting below the clock, that a Bible app controls directly. It refreshes on its own schedule (typically daily) without you touching anything.

Most people who search for a lock screen Bible verse wallpaper actually want one of two outcomes: a beautiful static image with a verse baked in, or a widget that keeps showing new verses automatically. Below covers both.

## Setting a Static Lock Screen Bible Verse Wallpaper (iPhone)

1. Get a verse image: screenshot one from a Bible app, design your own (see our [aesthetic Bible verse wallpaper guide](/resources/aesthetic-bible-verse-wallpaper) for fonts and palettes), or pull one from a dedicated app.
2. Save the image to your Photos app.
3. Long-press your Lock Screen and tap **Customize**.
4. Choose **Photos** as the wallpaper source and select your verse image.
5. Adjust framing, then tap **Done** and set it as your current Lock Screen.

For a fuller walkthrough covering Home Screen setup and Focus mode-specific wallpapers, see our [iPhone Bible verse wallpaper guide](/resources/iphone-bible-verse-wallpaper).

## Adding an Auto-Refreshing Verse Widget

If you want a verse that changes daily without you managing anything:

1. Long-press your Lock Screen and tap **Customize**.
2. Tap the widget area below the clock.
3. Search for a Bible app you've downloaded, like YouVersion, Heavenly, or BitBible.
4. Tap to add its widget.
5. Tap **Done**.

The widget refreshes on the app's own schedule, usually once a day, independent of whatever image you've set as your background wallpaper.

Several apps are built specifically around this feature. Our [best Bible verse lock screen apps comparison](/resources/best-bible-verse-lock-screen-apps) ranks seven of them by design quality, price, and how much control you get over which verses appear.

## What About FaithLock?

[FaithLock](/) doesn't put a widget on your lock screen. It's a different mechanism entirely, and it's worth being precise about what it actually does: FaithLock blocks distracting apps using Apple's Screen Time technology, and when you try to open one of those blocked apps, a verse from the complete BSB (Berean Standard Bible) library of 31,000+ verses appears. You read it and answer a short quiz question before the app unlocks.

That verse shows up at the moment you try to open a blocked app, not on your lock screen itself. Separately, FaithLock has a Home Screen widget that displays a verse of the day, which is different from a lock screen widget.

If what you want is passive Scripture exposure every time you glance at your lock screen, a dedicated lock screen widget app from the list above is the right tool. If what you want is something that interrupts the exact moment you're about to open Instagram or TikTok with a verse you have to actually engage with, that's what FaithLock is built for. They solve different problems, and plenty of people use both.

## Multiple Lock Screens for Different Moments

iOS lets you build more than one Lock Screen, each tied to a Focus mode. You could pair a peace-themed lock screen bible verse wallpaper with your Sleep Focus, and a strength-themed one with your Work Focus.

1. Set up a new Lock Screen (steps above), choosing a verse and wallpaper that fits the mood.
2. While customizing it, tap **Focus** and select which Focus mode activates it.
3. Repeat for each Focus mode you use regularly.

Your phone switches wallpapers automatically as your Focus mode changes throughout the day.

## On Android

Android's lock screen widget support varies more by manufacturer than iOS does. Stock Android and Pixel devices generally let you add widgets through **Settings > Display > Lock Screen**, if your device supports it at all. Samsung's One UI has its own lock screen widget system under **Settings > Lock Screen**.

For a static wallpaper: long-press an empty area of your home screen, tap **Wallpapers**, and choose a saved verse image, applying it to your lock screen specifically if your device separates lock screen and home screen wallpaper.

If your phone doesn't support native lock screen widgets, YouVersion's Android app is the most reliable option for a home screen widget, and a launcher like KWGT can extend widget placement further for phones that allow it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What's the difference between a lock screen Bible verse wallpaper and a widget?**
A wallpaper is a static background image you set once. A widget is a small block, usually below the clock, that an app refreshes automatically, typically once a day. You can use both together.

**Can I set a different Bible verse wallpaper for my lock screen and home screen?**
Yes, on iPhone. They're separate wallpaper slots, both configurable through Settings > Wallpaper or by long-pressing each screen directly.

**Does FaithLock have a lock screen widget?**
No. FaithLock's widget is a Home Screen widget showing a verse of the day. On the lock screen side, FaithLock instead shows a verse when you try to open a blocked app, using a Bible verse quiz as the unlock step.

**Which app gives the best lock screen Bible verse widget?**
Heavenly is generally regarded as the best-designed option, with BitBible offering a simpler, purpose-built alternative and YouVersion the most reliable free option. See our [full lock screen apps comparison](/resources/best-bible-verse-lock-screen-apps) for a detailed breakdown.

**Do lock screen Bible verse widgets work without internet?**
Most cache the current verse locally, so it still displays offline. Getting the next day's verse requires a brief connection to refresh.
