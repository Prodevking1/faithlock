## The Short Answer

To pray the Rosary: make the Sign of the Cross, say the Apostles' Creed on the crucifix, then an Our Father, three Hail Marys and a Glory Be on the opening beads. Announce the first mystery, pray an Our Father, ten Hail Marys and a Glory Be. Repeat for all five mysteries, then close with the Hail, Holy Queen.

That takes about twenty minutes. The rest of this page gives you every prayer in full, the mysteries assigned to each day of the week, and answers to the questions people actually get stuck on.

---

## What You Need

A rosary is a string of beads used to keep your place. A standard five decade rosary has a crucifix, then one bead, then three beads, then one bead, then a medal or centerpiece, and then five sets of ten small beads separated by single larger beads. Each set of ten is called a decade.

You do not need physical beads. Your fingers work. So does counting in your head. The beads exist so your hands can track the count while your mind stays on the mystery, which is the actual point of the prayer.

## How to Pray the Rosary Step by Step

The order below follows the steps published by the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops and by Catholic Answers (both checked 2026-08-16).

### 1. Make the Sign of the Cross

Hold the crucifix and pray:

> In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

### 2. Pray the Apostles' Creed

Still holding the crucifix:

> I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of heaven and earth, and in Jesus Christ, his only Son, our Lord, who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died and was buried; he descended into hell; on the third day he rose again from the dead; he ascended into heaven, and is seated at the right hand of God the Father almighty; from there he will come to judge the living and the dead.
> I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy catholic Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Amen.

### 3. On the first large bead, pray the Our Father

> Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

### 4. On the next three small beads, pray a Hail Mary on each

> Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

These three are traditionally offered for an increase in faith, hope and charity.

### 5. Pray the Glory Be

> Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit. As it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

### 6. Announce the first mystery

Say the name of the first mystery from today's set (the table below tells you which set). Pause for a moment on it. Many people read a short Scripture passage here.

### 7. On the next large bead, pray the Our Father

### 8. On the ten small beads, pray a Hail Mary on each

While your mouth says the Hail Mary, your mind stays on the mystery you just announced. This is the part beginners find strange and the part everything else exists to serve. The repetition is meant to occupy the surface of your attention so the scene can settle underneath it.

### 9. After the tenth Hail Mary, pray the Glory Be

### 10. Add the Fatima Prayer if you wish

> O my Jesus, forgive us our sins, save us from the fires of hell. Lead all souls to heaven, especially those most in need of thy mercy.

This one is customary rather than required. It comes from the 1917 Fatima apparitions and was added to common practice afterward, which is why some printed guides include it and older ones do not.

### 11. Repeat steps 6 through 10 for the remaining four mysteries

### 12. Pray the Hail, Holy Queen

> Hail, Holy Queen, Mother of mercy, our life, our sweetness, and our hope. To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve; to thee do we send up our sighs, mourning and weeping in this valley of tears. Turn then, most gracious advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us, and after this our exile, show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus. O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary.
>
> V. Pray for us, O holy Mother of God.
> R. That we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ.

### 13. Pray the closing prayer

> Let us pray. O God, whose only begotten son, by his life, death, and resurrection, has purchased for us the rewards of eternal life, grant, we beseech thee, that while meditating on these mysteries of the most holy rosary of the Blessed Virgin Mary, we may imitate what they contain and obtain what they promise, through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.

### 14. End with the Sign of the Cross

## The Mysteries of the Rosary by Day

Each set contains five events from the life of Christ and his mother. You pray one set per Rosary.

| Day | Mysteries |
|---|---|
| Monday | Joyful |
| Tuesday | Sorrowful |
| Wednesday | Glorious |
| Thursday | Luminous |
| Friday | Sorrowful |
| Saturday | Joyful |
| Sunday | Glorious (Joyful during Advent, Sorrowful during Lent) |

**The Joyful Mysteries**
1. The Annunciation
2. The Visitation
3. The Nativity
4. The Presentation in the Temple
5. The Finding in the Temple

**The Sorrowful Mysteries**
1. The Agony in the Garden
2. The Scourging at the Pillar
3. The Crowning with Thorns
4. The Carrying of the Cross
5. The Crucifixion and Death

**The Glorious Mysteries**
1. The Resurrection
2. The Ascension
3. The Descent of the Holy Spirit
4. The Assumption
5. The Coronation of Mary

**The Luminous Mysteries**
1. The Baptism of Christ in the Jordan
2. The Wedding Feast at Cana
3. Jesus' Proclamation of the Coming of the Kingdom of God
4. The Transfiguration
5. The Institution of the Eucharist

The Luminous Mysteries are newer than the other three sets. John Paul II proposed them in the 2002 apostolic letter *Rosarium Virginis Mariae*, listing them at paragraph 21 as the Baptism in the Jordan, the self-manifestation at Cana, the proclamation of the Kingdom, the Transfiguration and the institution of the Eucharist. At paragraph 38 he set out the weekly distribution above, and added that the arrangement "is not intended to limit a rightful freedom in personal and community prayer." Some Catholics pray the traditional three sets only. Neither choice is wrong.

## A Short Meditation for Each Mystery

If your mind goes blank when you announce a mystery, hold one line of Scripture instead of trying to picture the whole scene. Quotations here are from the New American Bible, Revised Edition (NABRE), the translation approved for use in the United States.

- **The Annunciation.** "Behold, I am the handmaid of the Lord. May it be done to me according to your word" (Luke 1:38).
- **The Visitation.** "Most blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb" (Luke 1:42).
- **The Nativity.** "And Mary kept all these things, reflecting on them in her heart" (Luke 2:19).
- **The Presentation.** "Now, Master, you may let your servant go in peace, according to your word, for my eyes have seen your salvation" (Luke 2:29-30).
- **The Agony in the Garden.** Not a scene to explain, a place to sit for two minutes.

The two lines of the Hail Mary that greet Mary are themselves Scripture: the angel's "Hail, favored one! The Lord is with you" (Luke 1:28) and Elizabeth's greeting at Luke 1:42.

## How Long the Rosary Takes

A five decade Rosary prayed at a normal pace runs about twenty minutes. Prayed quickly, closer to fifteen. Prayed slowly with a Scripture verse before each decade, closer to thirty.

If twenty minutes is not available, pray one decade. A single decade with attention is worth more than five decades raced through while you check the time. The Church has never treated the Rosary as an obligation with a minimum quota.

## Common Mistakes and Honest Difficulties

**Losing your place.** Everyone does. Pick up where you think you were and keep going. There is no penalty and no need to restart.

**Distraction.** Also everyone. The recurring advice from spiritual writers is not to fight distraction but to return, gently, as many times as it takes. Returning is the exercise.

**Feeling like it is vain repetition.** This is the most common objection, including from Catholics. The short answer is that the repeated words are a floor, not the building. You are not informing God. You are giving your body something to do so your attention can rest on one scene from the Gospel for two minutes at a time.

**Your phone.** If you pray with a rosary app, the notification that arrives during the third decade will take you out of it completely. Airplane mode before you start solves most of this. So does leaving the phone in another room and using actual beads.

## Praying the Rosary Without Getting Pulled Away

The practical obstacle for most people is not the prayers. It is that the twenty minutes never arrives, or arrives and gets eaten. Two things help.

First, attach the Rosary to something that already happens daily: the commute, the walk, the last twenty minutes before bed. A habit anchored to an existing habit survives; a habit that needs its own slot usually does not.

Second, put the phone out of reach. If you want the phone itself to enforce it, a screen time blocker can lock your usual time sinks during a fixed window. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do you pray the Rosary for beginners?**
Start with steps 1 through 5 above, then pray one decade instead of five: announce a mystery, pray an Our Father, ten Hail Marys and a Glory Be. Close with the Sign of the Cross. That takes four minutes and is a complete, legitimate prayer. Add decades as the prayers become familiar enough that you are not reading them off a screen.

**What mysteries do you pray on which day?**
Monday and Saturday are Joyful, Tuesday and Friday are Sorrowful, Wednesday and Sunday are Glorious, Thursday is Luminous. Sundays in Advent take the Joyful Mysteries and Sundays in Lent take the Sorrowful.

**How many beads are on a rosary?**
A standard five decade rosary has 59 beads: one for the Our Father at the start, three Hail Marys, then five decades of one Our Father bead and ten Hail Mary beads each. The crucifix and centerpiece are not counted as beads.

**Is the Fatima Prayer required?**
No. It is a widespread custom rather than part of the structure. The USCCB lists the steps without it and Catholic Answers lists it as step 10. Include it or leave it out.

**Can non-Catholics pray the Rosary?**
Nothing prevents it. The Our Father is from Matthew 6, the first half of the Hail Mary is Luke 1:28 and 1:42, and the mysteries are Gospel scenes. The intercessory second half of the Hail Mary is where Catholic and Protestant practice genuinely diverge, and it is worth knowing that rather than glossing over it.

---

## The Verdict

If you are starting from zero, do not try to hold all fourteen steps in your head. Learn the Hail Mary cold, pray one decade a day for two weeks, and let the rest assemble itself. The people who keep praying the Rosary years later are almost never the ones who started with a perfect twenty minute session; they are the ones who found four minutes that repeated.
