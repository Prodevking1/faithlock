2. **Say it before you scroll.** Before opening any app in the morning, read your chosen verse out loud. Start with God's voice, not the world's noise.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is addiction a sin or a disease?**
It involves both spiritual and physical components. Romans 7:15 describes the experience perfectly — doing what you don't want to do. Addiction needs both spiritual healing and often professional treatment. Don't choose one or the other.

**Can God heal addiction?**
Yes. John 8:36 says 'if the Son sets you free, you will be free indeed.' But freedom often comes through a process — counseling, support groups, accountability, medical help. God works through all of these.

**Is phone addiction a real addiction?**
Behavioral addiction to phones follows the same neurological patterns as substance addiction — dopamine, tolerance, withdrawal. If you can't put it down despite wanting to, that's addiction. Take it seriously.

**Where do I start if I think I'm addicted?**
Admit it. Tell someone. Seek professional help if needed. Set boundaries (delete apps, use blockers like FaithLock, charge your phone outside your bedroom). And pray — God's grace is sufficient for your weakness (2 Corinthians 12:9).
