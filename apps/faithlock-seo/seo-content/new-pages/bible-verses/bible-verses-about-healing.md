## How to Use These Verses Daily

1. **Pick one verse that resonates.** Don't try to absorb all ten. Choose the one that hit hardest and sit with it for a week.

2. **Say it before you scroll.** Before opening any app in the morning, read your chosen verse out loud. Start with God's voice, not the world's noise.

3. **Use technology to support your growth.** Tools like [FaithLock](/) can put Scripture between you and distracting apps, creating built-in moments to encounter God's Word throughout the day.

4. **Share what you're learning.** Text one verse to someone who needs it. When you give away what God gives you, it multiplies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does God still heal today?**
Yes. God's character doesn't change (Malachi 3:6). He healed in Scripture, and He heals now — through medicine, through prayer, through time, and sometimes through miracles.

**Why doesn't God always heal?**
This is one of the hardest questions in faith. Paul had a 'thorn in the flesh' God didn't remove (2 Corinthians 12:7-9). Sometimes God heals through the difficulty rather than removing it. Trust is required when answers aren't.

**Should I stop treatment and just pray?**
No. God works through doctors, medicine, and treatment. Seeking medical care is wisdom. Pray AND pursue treatment. They're not in competition.

**Can emotional wounds be healed?**
Yes. Psalm 147:3 says God heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds. Emotional healing is real, and it often comes through counseling, community, time, and Scripture.
