## How to Use These Verses Daily

1. **Pick one verse that resonates.** Don't try to absorb all ten. Choose the one that hit hardest and sit with it for a week.

2. **Say it before you scroll.** Before opening any app in the morning, read your chosen verse out loud. Start with God's voice, not the world's noise.

3. **Use technology to support your growth.** Tools like [FaithLock](/) can put Scripture between you and distracting apps, creating built-in moments to encounter God's Word throughout the day.

4. **Share what you're learning.** Text one verse to someone who needs it. When you give away what God gives you, it multiplies.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does God care about my physical health?**
Yes. 1 Corinthians 6:19-20 says your body is God's temple. 3 John 1:2 shows John praying for physical health alongside spiritual health. God cares about the whole you.

**Is it a sin to neglect my health?**
Your body is a stewardship from God. Neglecting it isn't a specific named sin, but it does conflict with the principle of honoring God with your body. Take care of what He gave you.

**How does screen time affect my health?**
Blue light disrupts sleep. Sedentary scrolling contributes to obesity. Constant phone use causes neck and back strain. The average person spends 7+ hours on screens daily. Reducing that benefits your health significantly.

**Should I pray for healing or see a doctor?**
Both. God heals through medicine and through miracles. Luke was a physician and a Gospel writer. Seeking medical care is wisdom, not lack of faith. Pray AND see your doctor.
