# FaithLock SEO Comparison Pages - Day 36

## Overview
Four comprehensive SEO-optimized comparison pages for FaithLock, a Christian app blocker for iOS. Each page is 1500-2000 words with rich, balanced comparison content.

## Files Created

### 1. bible-quiz-vs-bible-scanning-apps.md
- **Keyword:** bible quiz vs bible scanning apps
- **Word Count:** 2,400 words
- **Purpose:** Compares Bible quiz-based unlock mechanisms (FaithLock model) vs Bible scanning apps (Bible Mode model)
- **Key Comparison:** Knowledge testing vs physical Bible engagement, accessibility vs accountability

### 2. covenant-based-vs-time-based-blocking.md
- **Keyword:** covenant based vs time based blocking
- **Word Count:** 2,882 words
- **Purpose:** Compares FaithLock's 30-day covenant model vs traditional time-based scheduling apps
- **Key Comparison:** Commitment-driven vs automation-driven behavior change, skill building vs symptom removal

### 3. christian-vs-secular-screen-time-apps.md
- **Keyword:** christian vs secular screen time apps
- **Word Count:** 3,151 words
- **Purpose:** Compares faith-based tools (FaithLock, Holy Focus) vs secular apps (Freedom, Opal, One Sec)
- **Key Comparison:** Spiritual roots vs behavioral patterns, community accountability vs data-driven insights

### 4. faithlock-vs-screen-time-ios.md
- **Keyword:** FaithLock vs Screen Time iOS
- **Word Count:** 3,288 words
- **Purpose:** Compares FaithLock with Apple's built-in Screen Time feature
- **Key Comparison:** Free vs premium, environmental blocking vs spiritual motivation, scheduling vs covenant
