## Brand Voice (how FaithLock actually talks)

Words to use: scripture, faith, verse, unlock, redirect, intentional, habit, freedom, peace, reconnect, journey, grow, believers, community

Words to avoid: restrict, punish, control, monitor, surveillance, sin, guilt, shame, addiction (use "habit" instead), block (prefer "pause" or "redirect")

Tone: Warm, encouraging, non-judgmental, aspirational. Like a supportive friend at church, not a pastor from the pulpit.

## Origin Story Elements (for "about" mentions)

- FaithLock was born from a personal struggle — reaching for the phone before the Bible every morning
- The idea: "What if the phone itself became the reason you read Scripture?"
- Not about restriction. About redirection. "Yes, but first..."
- 15-second pause with a Bible verse is enough to disrupt the autopilot
- The mascot Judah (a lion) encourages and celebrates with users
